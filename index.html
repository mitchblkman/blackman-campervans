The Blackmans Philosophy

Where craft meets
the open road

Every Blackman's van begins with a conversation and ends with a vehicle that is entirely yours. Built by hand in our NSW workshop, each build reflects the landscape it was made for — rugged enough for the outback, refined enough for wherever life takes you.

We build around ten vans a year, and we intend to keep it that way. The limitation is the point. When you work with us, you get our full attention — not a production line.

Dual induction cooktop, Kings 85L upright fridge/freezer, timber benchtops and smart storage designed around how people actually cook on the road.

On-demand hot water, discreet wet bath or outdoor shower connection. Comfort wherever you are.
