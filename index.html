Where craft meets
the open road

Every Blackman campervan begins with a conversation and ends with a vehicle that is entirely yours. Built by hand in our NSW workshop, each build reflects the landscape it was made for — rugged enough for the outback, refined enough for wherever life takes you.

We build around ten vans a year, and we intend to keep it that way. The limitation is the point.

Craftsmanship

Every detail
considered

01

Off-Grid Electrical

LiFePO₂ lithium batteries, dual solar input, MPPT controller, and a full 240V inverter. Weeks of power without a plug.

02

Full Kitchen

Induction cooktop, 12V compressor fridge, oak benchtop, and storage designed around how people actually cook on the road.

03

Hot Shower

On-demand hot water, discreet wet bath or outdoor shower connection. Comfort that follows you everywhere.

04

Custom Cabinetry

Handbuilt linen-finish cabinetry with birch ply edges. Every drawer, door, and shelf designed for your specific needs.

05

Climate Control

Spray foam and rigid board insulation throughout. Designed for Australian conditions — cold highlands, coastal humidity, desert heat.

06

Compliance Ready

Full NSW registration, engineer certification, and dealer-licensed build process. You drive it the day it is handed over.
